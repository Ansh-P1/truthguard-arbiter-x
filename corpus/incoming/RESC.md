This notice is issued by the Skyline Telecom regulatory affairs office after a legal review of the recent billing changes. It records a decision of the executive committee and takes effect on the date stated below.

Billing Amendment No. 3 is hereby withdrawn with effect from 15 January 2026.

The billing policy that was in force before that amendment applies again from the same date. Care teams and retail partners should update scripts and notices accordingly. Questions should be sent to the policy owner for billing.
