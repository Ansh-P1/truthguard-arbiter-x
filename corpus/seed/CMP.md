This handbook summarises the obligations Skyline Telecom owes to its licence regulator and describes how each department demonstrates compliance. It is reviewed every year by the compliance committee and shared with all department heads. Audit findings are tracked until they are closed.

Call detail records must be retained for 180 days.

Departments that hold such records are responsible for confirming this period in their annual attestation to the compliance committee. Any request from a regulator for records must be routed through the compliance office.
