Welcome to the Skyline Telecom customer care FAQ. Here you will find short answers to the questions our agents hear most often, from changing a plan to understanding the lines on your monthly statement. This page is a convenience summary and is not a contract.

Q: What if I think my bill is wrong?
A: Check the itemised charges in the self-care app first. You can dispute a bill within 14 days.

Q: How do I change my plan?
A: Open the app, choose Plans, and pick the one you want. Changes take effect from the next billing cycle. For anything not covered here, please talk to a care agent through the app or at a retail store.
