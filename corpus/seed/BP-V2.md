This policy replaces the earlier billing policy and sets out how Skyline Telecom issues, collects and corrects postpaid bills for retail customers. Bills are generated every month and delivered through the self-care app and by email. Payment is due on the date printed on the bill, and late payments may attract a reconnection fee as listed in the tariff schedule.

Customers may raise a billing dispute within 30 days of the bill date.

Disputed amounts are placed on hold while the care team investigates, and customers are told the outcome in writing. Undisputed amounts must still be paid on time. This version also introduces a digital dispute form in the self-care app so that customers no longer need to visit a store.
