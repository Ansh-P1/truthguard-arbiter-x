To all authorised retail partners: this memo is a reminder about how to talk to walk-in customers about their bills and plan changes during the festive season. Please keep brochures stocked and make sure every counter has a working card reader.

Retail partners should inform customers they have 21 days to dispute a bill.

Partners are not authorised to settle disputes themselves; please raise a ticket through the partner portal and give the customer the reference number. Contact your regional sales manager if you need more brochures.
