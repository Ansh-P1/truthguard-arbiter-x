This FAQ answers common questions from prepaid customers. Prepaid balances are deducted in real time as you use data, calls and messages, and you can check your balance by dialling the short code or using the app. This page is a convenience summary and is not a contract.

Q: What if a deduction looks wrong?
A: Check your usage history in the app. Prepaid users must dispute charges within 7 days.

Q: Do I get a bill?
A: No, prepaid accounts do not receive monthly bills. You will see a receipt for every recharge in the app.
