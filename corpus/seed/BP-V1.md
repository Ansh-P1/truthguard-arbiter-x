This policy sets out how Skyline Telecom issues, collects and corrects postpaid bills for retail customers. Bills are generated every month and delivered through the self-care app and by email. Payment is due on the date printed on the bill, and late payments may attract a reconnection fee as listed in the tariff schedule. Customers who believe a charge is wrong should contact a care agent with the bill number and a short description of the problem.

Customers may raise a billing dispute within 14 days of the bill date.

Disputed amounts are placed on hold while the care team investigates, and customers are told the outcome in writing. Undisputed amounts must still be paid on time. This policy applies to all general retail postpaid accounts unless a separate agreement says otherwise.
