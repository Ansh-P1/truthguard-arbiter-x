This policy defines how Skyline Telecom protects customer and network data. It covers access control, encryption in transit and at rest, incident reporting and the handling of logs generated by network equipment. All employees and contractors must complete annual security training.

Call detail records are retained for 90 days.

After that period the records are securely deleted unless a lawful request requires them to be preserved. Access to records is limited to named staff and is logged. Exceptions must be approved by the chief information security officer in writing.
