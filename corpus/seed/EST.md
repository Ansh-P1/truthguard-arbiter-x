These terms govern the services Skyline Telecom provides to enterprise accounts, including bulk SIM provisioning, dedicated account management and consolidated invoicing. They apply in addition to the general terms of service and take precedence for enterprise customers where they differ.

Enterprise accounts may raise billing disputes within 60 days.

Each enterprise account has a named account manager who coordinates dispute handling and provides a monthly reconciliation report. Service credits, where agreed, are applied to the next consolidated invoice.
