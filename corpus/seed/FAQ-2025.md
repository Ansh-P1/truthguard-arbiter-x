Welcome to the refreshed Skyline Telecom customer care FAQ. These short answers cover privacy, support and everyday account questions. This page is a convenience summary and is not a contract.

Q: How long do you store my call history?
A: We keep your call records for 90 days.

Q: How can I reach support?
A: Our support team is available 24x7 on 198. You can also use the chat option in the self-care app at any time.

Q: Where can I read the full policies?
A: The full policy documents are available from your account manager or at any retail store.
