This amendment is issued by the Skyline Telecom regulatory affairs office following a review of customer complaint trends during the previous financial year. It takes effect on the first day of June 2025 and applies to all general retail postpaid accounts.

Section 4.1 of Billing Policy v2 is replaced in its entirety: the dispute period is extended to 45 days.

All other sections of the billing policy remain unchanged. Care teams must update their scripts and training material before the effective date, and the self-care app will show the new period on the dispute form. Questions about this amendment should be sent to the policy owner for billing.
